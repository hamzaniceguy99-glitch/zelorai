How we work

Vendor-neutral, and careful about safety

Zelorai sells no printers, filament or upgrades and takes nothing from any manufacturer. Most people need better calibration far more than a new machine, and that advice is hard to give when a shop is paying for it.

We also stay away from two things: modifications that disable thermal runaway protection or other safety features, and anything involving mains wiring. Resin and ABS get honest treatment on ventilation, gloves and disposal rather than a shrug.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Which printer should I buy?

We'll give you an honest recommendation for your budget and earn nothing from it. If you already own something decent, the answer is usually to calibrate it.

My first layer never sticks.

Almost always bed level, nozzle height or surface prep, in that order. Send a photo of the layer and we'll tell you which.

Do you cover resin printing?

Yes, including the parts people skip: ventilation, gloves, washing, curing and disposing of uncured resin properly.

Can you help me repair my printer?

We can diagnose and talk you through routine maintenance. We don't service machines, and we won't advise on mains wiring or on removing safety protections.

Can I sell what I print?

Often, but check the license on any model you didn't design and respect patents and trademarks. Designing your own avoids most of it, which is what the CAD program is for.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
